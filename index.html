Source and interpretation

World Development Indicators series IS.SHP.GOOD.TU measures container port traffic in twenty-foot equivalent units (TEU). Values are reported for economies, including some territories. This explorer excludes World Bank regional and income aggregates. A blank observation remains missing.

A matched pair requires a positive 2019 value and a reported comparison-year value. Coverage includes economies with no observations in this period, including landlocked economies. The figures describe the reporting subset and should not be interpreted as totals for the world or a whole region.
